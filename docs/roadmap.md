# Roadmap

## Shipped foundation

Single-user local workspace, intake, editable plan, persisted jobs, read-only SEO crawl, source-note-based video drafts, manual export/review, expiring draft schedules, stop switch and offline tests.

## Next: grounded research

- Search-provider interface with citations, freshness and quotas.
- GSC and GA4 official reporting adapters with scoped OAuth and clear attribution limits.
- Keyword/SERP research using configured official/licensed providers; no invented volume or difficulty.
- Reference-video ingestion with ownership/permission confirmation, transcription and timestamped visual analysis. The current transcript-notes field is not audiovisual analysis.
- Fact-check/source checklist and human review for all generated claims.

## Then: execution adapters

- PR-based website recommendations and changes; no direct edits to production.
- TTS and renderer interfaces with manifest provenance, original permitted assets and actual output validation.
- YouTube / Instagram OAuth adapters with content-hash approval, idempotent upload ledger, platform-specific disclosure and publication limits.
- Separate approvals for recurring research, paid generation and external publication; configurable monetary budget enforcement before enabling paid schedules.

## Before hosting

Authentication, encrypted credential store, user isolation, secure uploads, billing limits, task sandboxing, durable worker infrastructure and an externally audited permission boundary.

Work incrementally. Do not mark roadmap integrations connected merely because the UI can describe them.
